# Natal landing content guidelines

Use these as review heuristics, not rigid formulas or promises of conversion
uplift.

## Clarity and relevance

- Make the audience, problem, useful outcome, and next action understandable
  quickly in the first screen.
- Prefer plain, concrete language and short sentences. Remove internal jargon,
  abstractions, stacked qualifiers, and repeated claims.
- Align copy with the evaluated audience and their current problem. Do not
  invent traffic intent or a psychological profile when the source does not
  support one.
- Give each section one job and one clear thought sequence. Remove distractions
  that compete with the primary action, while preserving canonical template
  navigation and required legal/accessibility behavior.

## Outcome-first copy

- Lead headlines with the useful change the audience can understand. Do not
  force a fixed headline formula, timeframe, or objection into copy unless the
  source verifies it.
- Translate features into what the user can do, understand, avoid, or improve.
  Keep the underlying mechanism truthful and specific.
- Make steps concrete and low-effort. The next action should feel easy to
  understand without concealing real commitment or requirements.
- Use FAQ items to answer genuine decision-blocking questions. Do not create
  fictional objections merely to add length.

## Friction and trust

- Reduce reading, tapping, scrolling, and input that is not necessary for the
  chosen action. Ask only for information the real workflow requires.
- Place relevant trust information near the claim or action it supports.
- Use testimonials, counts, credentials, affiliations, security statements,
  scarcity, and deadlines only when the source provides verifiable facts.
- When no proof exists, say so honestly. Never rewrite an assumption, lesson,
  or owner comment as a customer result.

## Mobile review

- Treat 360 px as a first-class preview width and also inspect desktop.
- Keep interactive targets at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels and leave enough space
  to avoid accidental taps.
- Use clear hierarchy, readable line lengths, wrapping text, and collapsible FAQ
  details. Do not allow horizontal overflow.
- Keep the primary action reachable and visually clear without covering page
  content or relying on hover.

Do not cite unsupported conversion percentages, performance penalties, or
supposedly universal equations. Use only source-backed statements from the
approved Positioning revision and verified results from this product.
