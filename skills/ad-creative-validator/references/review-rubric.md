# Complete creative review rubric

Inspect the actual attached render at both full size and imagined feed-thumbnail
size. A successful creative passes all of these dimensions as one system.

## Blocking checks

1. The exact approved offer and CTA are present, readable, untruncated, and not
   weakened by surrounding copy.
2. No unsupported proof, testimonial, rating, outcome, guarantee, urgency,
   scarcity, sensitive targeting, watermark, or unapproved identity appears.
3. The hook is specific, immediately understandable, and strong enough to earn
   a half-second pause from the intended audience.
4. Hook, body copy, offer, CTA, and visual tell one coherent story with one
   dominant message.
5. The visual embodies or completes the hook instead of decorating it; emotion,
   human tension or meaningful symbolism, and industry context agree.
6. Crop and focal point preserve the important subject, face, gesture, product,
   or symbolic action. Text and shapes do not cover meaningful content.
7. Every visible component is intentionally placed inside the platform safe
   area. Nothing collides, clips, overflows, crowds, or looks accidentally
   aligned.
8. Hierarchy is obvious: hook first, evidence or support second when useful,
   offer and CTA as the next action, Natal as supporting identity.
9. Typography is readable on a small screen: sufficient size, contrast,
   line-breaking, spacing, and restrained line count. Background treatments
   support rather than fight the text.
10. Color, logo, type, shapes, and image treatment are consistent with the
    supplied brand kit and feel deliberately composed rather than templated.
11. Caption and alt text accurately match the final visual and approved claim;
    the caption retains the exact offer.
12. The result feels native to its declared placement and polished enough for
    owner review without relying on performance data.

## Scores

Score each from 1 to 10: stop-scroll strength, hook quality, message clarity,
visual/copy fit, composition, hierarchy, legibility, brand consistency,
credibility, placement fit, and accessibility. Do not average away a weak
dimension. Every score must reach 8 before approval.

## Revision quality

Comments must name the observed problem and the intended correction. The
replacement recipe must implement the comments, not merely restate them. Prefer
the fewest components that create a clear hierarchy, but add a useful body,
badge, shape, product, media, or other catalog frame when the creative cannot
succeed through edits to existing components alone.
