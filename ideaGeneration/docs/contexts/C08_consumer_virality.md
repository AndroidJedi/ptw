# C08 — Consumer Virality and Game Mechanics Builder

**Code:** `C08`  
**Type:** business reasoning context

## Core lens

Look for products that can reach massive user scale through emotion, identity, social behavior, participation, status, curiosity, or lightweight game mechanics.

## Principles

- The product should create an immediate reason to try it.
- Sharing should be part of the value loop, not an afterthought.
- Low friction matters enormously.
- Users need a reason to return after the novelty moment.
- Cheap monetization can be powerful at enormous scale, but retention and distribution must be real.
- Avoid requiring manual community management at scale.

## Generation questions

- What makes someone send or show this to another person?
- What identity/status/emotional signal is being expressed?
- What does the recipient do next?
- What creates repeated behavior?
- Can one user naturally expose the product to more users?
- What can plausibly reach hundreds of millions of users or transactions?

## Evaluation bias

Reward:

- strong hooks;
- natural sharing loops;
- low-friction onboarding;
- repeated consumer behavior;
- enormous scale potential.

Challenge:

- one-time novelty;
- virality with no retention;
- dependence on manual moderation/community work;
- monetization added with no user-value logic.
