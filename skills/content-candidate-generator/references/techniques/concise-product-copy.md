# Concise product copy

Use a compact hierarchy:

1. one concrete promise or customer moment;
2. one sentence that makes the mechanism visible;
3. exact offer;
4. exact CTA with supplied reassurance.

Prefer verbs, actors, timing, and small sequences over abstract benefit nouns.
Remove repeated meaning. Text-only output still includes caption and alt text as
usable structured fields, but it requests no media and returns no visual
components.
