# Bounded research — static B2B creative

Recorded: 2026-08-31
Scope: one primary-source pass to choose the next same-Brief advertising-image
experiment after `exp-20260831-006`. No market sizing, campaign, targeting,
traffic, or optimization work was performed.

## Sources and observations

1. LinkedIn and MAGNA Media Trials, *The B2B Renaissance* summary, 2024:
   <https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/research-and-insights/new-research-the-creative-tactics-that-drive-better-b2b-ad-performance-on-linkedin>
   - The controlled study covered 67 single-image and video Sponsored Content
     ads and more than 1,700 business decision makers.
   - Both display and video improved brand outcomes in the study. Ads perceived
     as creative produced stronger consideration and engagement than the
     ordinary B2B ads in the tested set.
   - Actionable distinction: another rational feature diagram has low expected
     information gain. Test a more original visual idea while keeping the
     supplied product mechanism explicit.

2. LinkedIn and VidMob, *Creative Trends: Looking Back at 2024 Through a Data
   Lens*, 2024:
   <https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/marketing-collective/linkedin-creative-trends-looking-back-at-2024-through-a-data-lens>
   - The reported analysis covered 13,600 creative assets and 2.9 billion
     impressions.
   - High-contrast visuals were associated with 41% higher static engagement;
     neutral backgrounds with clear text contrast were associated with 15%
     higher click-through rates for tech brands.
   - Actionable distinction: use one bold Natal-cyan focal element against a
     neutral field, then keep protected copy on a calm, high-contrast surface.

3. LinkedIn B2B Institute and MediaScience, *Better, Bolder B2B Branding*:
   <https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/resources/b2b-institute/b2branding-whitepaper-final.pdf>
   - In the study of 770 US professionals, an ad was on-screen for 12.3 seconds
     on average but received 3.7 seconds of attention, fragmented across an
     average 2.4 focus switches.
   - The authors recommend short, succinct brand-centric messages, avoiding
     elaborate concepts that require viewers to reconstruct missed steps.
   - Actionable distinction: one glance must reveal the object, the customer
     outcome, Natal, the offer, and the action. Do not build another multi-step
     route or status taxonomy.

4. LinkedIn B2B Institute, *The CMO Scorecard*:
   <https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/resources/b2b-institute/cmo-scorecard-whitepaper.pdf>
   - The report recommends using distinctive brand assets early, often,
     centrally, and consistently because creative can be remembered without
     correct brand attribution.
   - Actionable distinction: the visual idea must carry Natal's cyan and the
     approved Natal mark must be visible in the primary scan path, not appended
     as an unrelated footer.

## Conclusion

Pre-register one `object_metaphor@1` candidate: a familiar physical appointment
card or desk-calendar object is visibly carried around a return loop made from
one bold cyan ribbon. The exact Natal logo, concise outcome headline, supported
mechanism line, protected offer, and protected CTA are composited
deterministically. The generated bitmap contains no people, faces, text, logos,
screens, UI, metrics, or proof claims.

This is a testable, materially different direction. It trades the current
set's dark diagrams and feature explanation for a branded tangible object and
one customer outcome, while retaining the approved Brief's honest mechanism.
It is a research-informed hypothesis, not a predicted winner.
