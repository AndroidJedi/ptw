# Marketing Positioning evidence lineage

The owner idea is stored as a permanent Source before synthesis. It is the sole
factual Source in the active Positioning flow. The selected country and market
language shape the requested context but do not establish facts.

Each generated factual statement may cite the owner Source UUID only when the
idea directly supports it. Intended capabilities remain described as intended
unless the owner supplied evidence that they exist. Category choice, audience
narrowing, competitive alternatives, jobs, pains, gains, emotional rewards,
and other market conclusions carry no Source UUID and are marked
`assumption: true` unless the owner idea directly states them.

DataForSEO and other external research providers are not called. Model
knowledge is never represented as research or evidence. Metrics, proof,
testimonials, limitations, and competitive facts cannot be invented. Bridge,
schema-validation, or quality-gate failure remains a durable failed attempt.
