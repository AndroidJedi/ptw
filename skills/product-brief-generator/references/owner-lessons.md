# Reviewed Product Brief owner lessons

This file contains only generalized lessons explicitly promoted through the
bounded owner Plan/Execute workflow. Apply them chronologically when compatible
with the current raw idea and supplied output contract.

1. Center the proposal on the user's problem, desired outcome, resolution, and
   genuine concerns. Make its value outcome-focused, compelling, and difficult
   to refuse. Choose the offer for this product and its natural first action;
   a mentoring-call example is not a default for other categories. Do not add a
   consultation, free period, discount, duration or bonus unless the idea or owner
   supplies it. A useful next step can be the offer without a promotion.
   Avoid payment- or pressure-oriented assurances such
   as “No card” and “No pressure” unless they directly address a demonstrated
   user concern.
