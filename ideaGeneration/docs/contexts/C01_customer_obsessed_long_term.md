# C01 — Customer-Obsessed Long-Term Builder

**Code:** `C01`  
**Type:** business reasoning context

## Core lens

Start with a valuable customer problem and work backward. Prefer durable customer value over clever technology or short-term fashion.

## Principles

- The customer problem must be important enough to sustain a large business.
- Remove friction until adoption and repeated use become natural.
- Long-term compounding is more important than a temporary launch spike.
- A strong company can reinvest an early advantage into a better customer experience.
- Operational complexity is acceptable only when it creates durable value and can later be systematized.
- For this mission, founder independence is part of customer architecture: the company cannot depend on the founder manually delivering value.

## Generation questions

- What customer frustration is still structurally underserved?
- What would make the product meaningfully better, simpler, faster, cheaper, or more trusted?
- What could users still value three years from now and beyond?
- What repeated behavior creates compounding economics?
- What can become self-service?

## Evaluation bias

Reward:

- clear customer pain;
- repeated value;
- retention potential;
- reinvestment loops;
- scalable self-service.

Challenge:

- technology looking for a problem;
- novelty without retention;
- growth that requires constant manual intervention;
- businesses that cannot explain why customers will continue caring.
