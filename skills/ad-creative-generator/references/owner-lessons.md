# Reviewed Ad Creative owner lessons

This file contains only generalized lessons explicitly promoted through the
bounded owner Plan/Execute workflow. Apply them chronologically when compatible
with the current approved Product Brief and v1 output contract.

1. Prefer short, curiosity-driving questions or emotionally specific headlines
   over explanatory, research-like statements. Ensure the image visibly
   embodies the headline's core tension or action, such as doubt, commitment,
   pressure, determination, or forward movement. Reject generic device-checking
   or fatigued imagery that contradicts or merely decorates the message. Favor
   visuals that complete the story, and make the headline distinctive rather
   than cliche.

2. When relevant, prefer authentic photographs of real human interaction, such
   as two strangers in conversation. Preserve the tension and immediacy of a
   strong conversational image. Avoid corporate abstractions such as “Clear
   accountability” when the visual is human and emotionally charged; use a
   direct, natural headline that complements rather than wastes the photograph.
   Creative #2 owner scores: stop-scroll 2/10, curiosity 2/10, emotion 3/10,
   visual fit 9/10.

3. Clearly communicate the customer’s pain and make the product’s solution
   immediately understandable. When relevant, visually demonstrate the
   solution with a phone showing the app interface. Preserve strong imagery
   while improving problem–solution clarity.
