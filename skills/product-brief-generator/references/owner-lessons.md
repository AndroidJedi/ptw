# Reviewed Product Brief owner lessons

This file contains only generalized lessons explicitly promoted through the
bounded owner Plan/Execute workflow. Apply them chronologically when compatible
with the current raw idea and v1 output contract.

1. Center the proposal on the user's problem, desired outcome, resolution, and
   genuine concerns. Make its value outcome-focused, compelling, and difficult
   to refuse. Prefer the clear, lower-friction offer “Free 15-minute Mentor
   call” over vague “easy-access” framing or a longer “Free commitment plan and
   30-minute setup call.” Avoid payment- or pressure-oriented assurances such
   as “No card” and “No pressure” unless they directly address a demonstrated
   user concern.
