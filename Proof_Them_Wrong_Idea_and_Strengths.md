# Proof Them Wrong (PTW)

## Core Idea

**Proof Them Wrong (PTW)** is a social platform built around one
powerful human emotion:

> **The desire to prove other people wrong.**

Instead of posting random content, every participant publicly commits to
a meaningful goal and documents the journey.

Examples: - Build a startup - Lose 30 kg - Become a professional
athlete - Learn a new profession - Recover after failure - Write a book

The project page becomes a living timeline containing: - Motivation
("Why am I doing this?") - Progress updates - Photos and videos -
Evidence of progress - Community discussion

The audience is encouraged not only to support, but also to challenge
the participant.

Criticism becomes fuel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why it is different

PTW is not another social network.

Twitter/X is about opinions. Instagram is about lifestyle. LinkedIn is
about careers.

PTW is about **transformation**.

Every profile tells a story with a beginning, obstacles, critics, and
(hopefully) victory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strong Points

## 1. Extremely emotional

The motivation already exists.

People naturally want to prove: - parents - ex-partners - managers -
classmates - society

The product amplifies an existing emotion instead of creating an
artificial one.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Built-in storytelling

Every participant creates a real story.

Beginning → struggle → progress → result.

People love following stories much more than isolated posts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Viral by design

Participants naturally share their page because they want: -
supporters - critics - accountability - recognition

External sharing becomes part of the product.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Criticism is valuable

Unlike most social networks, disagreement increases engagement.

Comments such as:

"I don't think you'll make it."

are not necessarily negative.

They become motivation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Long-term retention

Projects last: - weeks - months - sometimes years

This creates natural recurring engagement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Strong identity

A participant is remembered by one mission instead of thousands of
unrelated posts.

This makes profiles memorable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Discovery is interesting

Visitors discover:

-   ambitious people
-   unusual ideas
-   transformations
-   unexpected winners

Watching unknown people become successful is inherently engaging.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. High-quality content

Content is naturally filtered.

Only people actually working toward something continue posting.

Low-effort content dies quickly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Social accountability

Public commitment increases the chance of success.

The platform becomes an accountability partner.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Universal audience

The concept works for almost any goal:

-   business
-   fitness
-   education
-   recovery
-   art
-   sports
-   personal challenges

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why it can spread

The product has multiple sharing loops:

-   Participants share their profile.
-   Critics invite others to watch.
-   Supporters invite friends.
-   Success stories inspire new participants.

Every successful project becomes marketing for the platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
