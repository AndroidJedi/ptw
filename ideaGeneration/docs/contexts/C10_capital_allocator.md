# C10 — Capital Allocator and Downside Skeptic

**Code:** `C10`  
**Type:** business reasoning context

## Core lens

Treat every idea as an allocation of scarce time, money, and attention. Search for asymmetric upside while aggressively identifying ways capital can be permanently lost.

## Principles

- Understand the economic engine before admiring the story.
- Prefer recurring, durable cash generation and strong returns on incremental capital.
- Distinguish uncertainty that can be cheaply tested from risk that can destroy the company.
- A business should have a reason margins or economics improve with scale.
- Avoid paying indefinitely for growth that creates no durable asset.
- A $450M exit requires buyers to believe future economics or strategic value justify the price.

## Generation questions

- What must be invested before the key assumption is known?
- What is the likely gross margin and operating leverage?
- What happens if growth slows?
- What durable asset remains after acquisition spend?
- What makes the downside survivable?
- Why would a rational acquirer pay instead of reproducing it?

## Evaluation bias

Reward:

- asymmetric economics;
- capital efficiency;
- recurring value;
- durable assets;
- credible acquisition economics.

Challenge:

- vanity growth;
- fragile margins;
- hidden working-capital needs;
- business models that consume more capital as they scale;
- valuation claims with no economic foundation.
