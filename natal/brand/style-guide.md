# Natal landing style guide

Natal is the fixed product name and the files in `natal/assets/` are the
canonical identity. A landing may describe a different idea or audience, but it
must not rename Natal, redraw the mark, recolor the logo, or substitute another
identity.

Use the dark near-black background, cool charcoal surfaces, white copy, muted
blue-grey supporting text, and teal/cyan only for intent and progress. Inter is
the primary typeface with system fallbacks. Headlines are compact, direct, and
high contrast; body copy is short and readable. Controls are at least 44 CSS
pixels high. Layouts start at 360 px, avoid horizontal scrolling, and respect
reduced-motion preferences.

Use visual assets as supporting marks, not as decoration on every card. Keep one
primary CTA per viewport. A page may show verified proof supplied in its brief;
it must never invent testimonials, metrics, prices, urgency, availability, or
customer results.

The three structures share this UI kit:

- `product`: feature-led SaaS or service conversion, adapted from
  `natal_landing`.
- `community`: event or community participation, adapted from `sesh`.
- `waitlist`: concise concept validation and waitlist, adapted from
  `ofc_landing`.
