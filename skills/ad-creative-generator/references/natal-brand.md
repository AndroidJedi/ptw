# Natal Ad identity

Natal is the immutable umbrella product name for every PTW project and Ad
Creative. Do not invent, retain, or substitute another brand identity. A Brief
may describe a different product, service, or audience; present it as Natal's
current offer without changing the Brief's approved facts, promise, CTA, or
offer.

Follow the canonical source in `natal/brand/style-guide.md` and
`natal/brand/brand.json`:

- canonical logo: `natal/assets/logo-natal.png`; never redraw, recolor, or
  replace it;
- primary typeface: Inter with Helvetica Neue, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks;
- near-black `#0c0e12`, charcoal `#181c25`/`#202531`, white `#f4f6fa`, and
  muted blue-grey `#a3adbd` form the base palette;
- cyan `#43bdd3` and light cyan `#87d0dd` mark intent and action;
- headlines are compact, direct, high-contrast, and visually specific;
- the logo is a supporting identity mark, not decorative filler.

The deterministic renderer applies the canonical font, logo, palette, offer,
and CTA. Model output must plan enough subject separation and crop-safe negative
space for that overlay; it must not add font, color, or logo fields to the
strict v1 contract.
