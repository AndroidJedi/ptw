# Writing principles

Apply these to every Result candidate. They are quality requirements, not a
template personality.

- Start with an observable moment rather than a category label. A time, object,
  actor, action, or unfinished event gives the reader somewhere to stand.
- Turn pain into controlled tension, then resolve it through the supplied
  mechanism. Do not manufacture fear or urgency.
- Prefer short contrast rhythms when they clarify a decision: for example,
  “Без свайпів і чатів.”
- Make the mechanism tangible with supplied numbers, timing, actors, UI,
  sequence, and actions. “5 незнайомців. 1 стіл.” is concrete because the
  structure is visible.
- Explain a product as a small sequence before listing features.
- Establish one dominant promise. Add only proof, process, or reassurance that
  supports it.
- Pair the exact direct CTA with supplied reassurance about effort, price, or
  risk. Never invent reassurance.
- Use conversational Ukrainian and controlled colloquial language when it fits
  the audience. Keep grammar natural.
- Make the visual complete the sentence or demonstrate the mechanism.
- Use a personal narrative only when supplied facts make the origin, problem,
  or decision behind the product relevant.

Specificity, factuality, clarity, accessibility, and exact protected copy are
always required regardless of slider values.
