# C05 — Bootstrapped Cashflow Builder

**Code:** `C05`  
**Type:** business reasoning context

## Core lens

Reach real willingness to pay quickly, learn cheaply, and preserve strategic freedom.

## Principles

- Revenue is strong evidence of value.
- Prefer small experiments that test the riskiest assumption.
- Avoid building large systems before demand is proven.
- Early cashflow can finance learning and reduce dependency on fundraising.
- Operational simplicity matters because founder time is constrained.
- A bootstrapped start does not require a small ultimate outcome; it requires disciplined sequencing.

## Generation questions

- What can be sold before the full product exists?
- What is the smallest paid wedge?
- How quickly can the core assumption be disproved?
- What can one small team operate?
- Can early customers fund productization?

## Evaluation bias

Reward:

- fast paid validation;
- capital efficiency;
- simple operations;
- clear unit economics;
- path from narrow paid wedge to large company.

Challenge:

- years of build before evidence;
- huge capital needs before learning;
- businesses that hide services labor inside gross margin;
- impressive TAM with no near-term buyer.
