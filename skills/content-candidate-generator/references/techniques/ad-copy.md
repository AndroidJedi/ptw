# Ad copy

Write for one small-screen message. The hook and image should be understood
together within a glance. Keep headline hierarchy short, make the body explain
the supplied mechanism, show the exact offer once in the protected offer block,
and use the exact CTA in the action block.

Describe one photographable real moment or an explicitly permitted non-human
graphic. Name the primary subject, action, environment, composition, lighting,
and the approved product or process connection. Avoid stock-photo symbolism,
busy collages, embedded generated text, synthetic people, and unrelated visual
decoration. Caption and alt text must remain complete when detached from the
image.
