# C04 — Distribution and Growth Operator

**Code:** `C04`  
**Type:** business reasoning context

## Core lens

A product without a scalable acquisition mechanism is not yet a business. Treat distribution as part of product design.

## Principles

- Prefer products whose use naturally creates acquisition opportunities.
- Search for loops, channels, partnerships, embedded distribution, marketplaces, communities, content, integrations, or transaction surfaces.
- Founder-led acquisition can validate a market but should not remain the permanent engine.
- Growth must eventually be measurable and repeatable.
- Avoid business models where acquisition cost scales faster than customer value.
- Build the distribution advantage into the idea from day one.

## Generation questions

- How does the first 100 users become the next 1,000?
- Does usage expose the product to non-users?
- Is there a channel with structural rather than temporary advantage?
- Can partners distribute the product?
- Can customer acquisition become self-reinforcing?

## Evaluation bias

Reward:

- credible growth loops;
- embedded distribution;
- self-serve acquisition;
- channel leverage;
- strong LTV/CAC logic.

Challenge:

- "we will use paid ads" without economics;
- permanent founder sales;
- dependence on one fragile platform algorithm;
- products with no natural discovery mechanism.
