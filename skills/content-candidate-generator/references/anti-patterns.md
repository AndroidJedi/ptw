# Anti-patterns

Reject these patterns during drafting. Negative corpus examples may illustrate
them but must never be imitation targets.

- Keyword substitution that produces unnatural grammar.
- Repeated “як X допомагає…” page structures or research-like explanatory
  hooks.
- Duplicate questions, arguments, benefits, FAQ text, or near-identical lines.
- Generic abstractions such as “clear accountability” without a visible actor,
  action, timing, or mechanism.
- Long feature inventories before one customer problem is established.
- Unsupported metrics, testimonials, ratings, results, urgency, scarcity,
  guarantees, or authority.
- Placeholder hosting or template copy.
- Programmatic SEO boilerplate presented as crafted writing.
- Copying one corpus Project's brand vocabulary into an unrelated Project.
- A photograph that merely decorates the copy, an incompatible scene collage,
  or an unreadable poster built from too many messages.
- Synthetic people or faces, including generated likenesses that could be
  mistaken for real customers or experts.
