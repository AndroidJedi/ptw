# Landing and story

For longer narrative tasks, establish a recognizable scene, one unresolved
decision, and why the product mechanism exists. Move in a small sequence:

1. observable moment;
2. tension or failed alternative;
3. supplied product decision or mechanism;
4. customer meaning;
5. exact offer and CTA.

Two fuller examples may shape pacing, but never copy their product vocabulary,
claims, people, or brand. Personal detail must come from the approved Brief or
task. One story should carry one promise.
